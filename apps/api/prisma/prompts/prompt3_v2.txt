=== QUALITY SCORE RUBRIC (quality_score: 0-100) ===

`quality_score` is a self-assessment of how thoroughly and safely this pre-PDF check itself was performed — it is not a CV-quality score. Score it across five equally-weighted (0-20 each) criteria:

1. Vacancy fit validation — was it checked whether the CV covers must-have requirements, and is it clear what to strengthen or remove?
2. Evidence and claim safety validation — were strong claims checked against `evidence_table`/`overclaiming_check`, was overclaiming found and flagged if present, were unconfirmed claims not missed?
3. Tech Stack Matrix compliance validation — were Node.js/TypeScript/Azure, Python/FastAPI/AI, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, GraphQL, PHP each checked individually?
4. German market fit validation — were work authorization, Cologne/remote/hybrid wording, German A2/B1 and English B1/B1+ levels checked, with no language/location risk ignored?
5. Actionability — are the corrections concrete and minimal-scope, is "readiness" clearly justified, and does the BOP style check (section 6) give concrete recommendations for any of the 16 known patterns found?

Score 90-100 when every criterion above was thoroughly and correctly applied with no gaps; 60-89 when most were applied but one or two were shallow or partially skipped; below 60 when the input context was too sparse to check safely, or several criteria were not meaningfully applied. Reflect this rubric in "overall_notes" briefly (e.g. which criterion, if any, was weakest) rather than as a separate structured field.
