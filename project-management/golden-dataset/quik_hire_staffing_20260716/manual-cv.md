### EGZ applicability decision

The correct criterion is not simply whether the brand is a “German company.” EGZ should be considered only where the hiring arrangement creates an employment relationship in Germany for which the employer handles German payroll and social-security contributions. This can include:

- a German employer or German legal entity;
- a foreign company employing the candidate through a German branch or German payroll setup;
- an Employer of Record acting as the legal employer in Germany;
- another arrangement that results in a sozialversicherungspflichtige Beschäftigung in Germany.

A foreign remote contractor agreement, freelance/B2B contract or project-based service contract is not enough.

For this vacancy, the listing says `Contract`, pays in USD per hour and does not identify a German employing entity, German payroll or an EOR. Therefore:

**EGZ eligibility unclear — not added to CV content.**

Use this wording in future analysis:

> EGZ may be relevant when the role is offered as sozialversicherungspflichtige employment in Germany through a German employer, German legal entity, German payroll setup or an Employer of Record acting as the legal employer in Germany. It should not be added for freelance, B2B or foreign contractor arrangements unless German employment and payroll are explicitly confirmed.
