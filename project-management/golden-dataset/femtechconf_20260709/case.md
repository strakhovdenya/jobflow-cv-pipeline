About FemTechConf

FemTechConf is one of Europe’s largest communities for women and allies in technology, with more than 450,000 members globally across software engineering, product, data, AI, cloud, cybersecurity, leadership, business, investment, and innovation.



We work with leading organisations to help build more diverse, innovative, and inclusive technology teams while creating access to opportunities for women and underrepresented talent across the technology industry.



Through our community, events, talent network, and partnerships, we help connect talented candidates with companies that are actively hiring and investing in the next generation of technology teams.



FemTechConf also organises leading Women in Tech events across Europe, including the Women in Tech EMEA Summit in Berlin and the Women in Tech London Summit, bringing together technical teams, hiring managers, founders, senior leaders, and partner companies from across the technology ecosystem.
