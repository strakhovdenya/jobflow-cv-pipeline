We are looking for a Senior Full Stack Engineer to join our dynamic R&D Team, where you will own high-scale features end-to-end across our FinOps platform and AWS billing engine — contributing directly to our mission of empowering fast-paced startups.



This is an agentic engineering role. It is not a traditional coding role. You will generate the vast majority of your production code with AI, and your value comes from the deep technical judgment you apply on top of it: guiding the model, critiquing its output, and shipping solutions that are high-quality, maintainable, and future-proof. We’re looking for someone excited that the craft of software is changing, and who wants to operate at (and help define) the frontier of it.



You will work with: Node.js/TypeScript (NestJS, Fastify, Prisma ORM) + React (TanStack, TipTap, Material UI, Untitled UI) + PostgreSQL.

If you are interested in this opportunity, please submit your CV in English.




Key Responsibilities: 


Own features end-to-end — from vague stakeholder ask to production deployment, including discovery, design, implementation, testing, rollout, and iteration.
Refine requirements proactively using the Value, Usability, Feasibility, Viability (VUFV) framework. Push back on weak requirements, propose better solutions than the one originally requested, and keep a tight feedback loop with stakeholders.
Generate your code with AI (we provide top-tier AI tooling — currently Claude Opus 4.7 in Cursor). Your job is to direct the model, review its output with a senior engineer’s eye, and take full ownership of what ships — including architecture, performance, security, and long-term maintainability.
Build and evolve our AI workflow — don’t just use it. Stay on the frontier of new models, tools, agents, and techniques; codify what works into our setup (rules, prompts, tooling, MCPs); and share it so the whole team levels up with you.
Solve deep technical problems across FinOps processes and AWS billing calculations.
Ship scalable, maintainable, well-tested, well-documented code — and create and maintain the system design and implementation docs that go with it.
