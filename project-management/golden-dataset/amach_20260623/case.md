About us:

Amach is an industry-leading technology driven company with headquarters located in Dublin and remote teams in UK and Europe.

Our blended teams of local and nearshore talent are optimised to deliver high quality and collaborative solutions.

Founded in 2013, Amach was created to solve a specific problem in aviation: too much complexity, too little usable intelligence. We help airlines modernise their operating model using cloud, data and Al-delivered by teams with deep aviation domain expertise.

Our goal is to maximize airlines' operational efficiency by optimizing resource use, reduce costs and increase customer experience and satisfaction.

 
We're hiring a Full Stack Developer to join a growing engineering team responsible for delivering and evolving a large-scale eCommerce platform. This role will play a key part in shaping the future of platform experience, working across both frontend and backend systems to deliver new customer-facing features and platform enhancements. 

Initially, the role will be more frontend-focused, supporting an ambitious product roadmap and helping to improve the customer experience. Over time, there will be opportunities to contribute to backend services, integrations, and platform development as the business expands it's eCommerce capabilities. 

This is an excellent opportunity for an engineer who enjoys working across the full technology stack, collaborating closely with product and engineering teams, and contributing throughout the software development lifecycle - from building and deploying solutions through to ongoing support and optimisation. 

Required Experience:

3+ years experience as a Full Stack Developer or Software Engineer
Strong experience with TypeScript and/or modern JavaScript 
Experience building applications using Node.js 
Frontend development experience with modern web technologies and frameworks 
Experience using monitoring and observability tools such as DataDog
Experience developing and supporting production applications 
Understanding of cloud platforms, ideally AWS 
Experience working within Agile development environments 
Strong problem-solving skills and a collaborative mindset 
Ability to work effectively within a distributed, cross-functional team 
Key responsibilities & duties include:

Develop and enhance customer-facing features across the eCommerce platform 
Build scalable, maintainable applications using TypeScript and Node.js

Support integrations with suppliers, fulfilment partners, and order management systems
