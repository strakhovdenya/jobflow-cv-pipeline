Requirements

4 + years of hands-on Full Stack development experience, with a track record of delivering and maintaining production web applications.
Proficient in JavaScript / TypeScript with a strong understanding of object-oriented and component-based design principles and the ability to produce clean, modular code.
Solid front-end expertise using modern frameworks — React in particular — complemented by strong HTML5, CSS3, and responsive-layout skills.
Proven back-end capability in designing and implementing RESTful and/or GraphQL APIs and services using Node.js, .NET/C#, or Python, including familiarity with microservices patterns.
Confident working with databases — both relational (PostgreSQL, SQL Server) and document-oriented (MongoDB) — with strong SQL proficiency and data-modelling aptitude.

Comfortable operating in Agile teams with a demonstrated habit of owning features from inception to release and beyond.
Effective communicator who can bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation, engaging confidently with both technical and non-technical audiences.
Desirable extras: experience with Docker/Kubernetes, event-driven or message-based architectures, automated testing strategies, AI-assisted development tools, and building products underpinned by AI/ML or large-scale, data-intensive systems.
