About FemTechConf

FemTechConf is one of Europe’s largest communities for women and allies in technology, with more than 450,000 members globally across software engineering, product, data, AI, cloud, cybersecurity, leadership, business, investment, and innovation.

We work with leading organisations to help build more diverse, innovative, and inclusive technology teams while creating access to opportunities for women and underrepresented talent across the technology industry.



Through our community, events, talent network, and partnerships, we help connect exceptional candidates with companies that are actively hiring and investing in diverse technology talent.

FemTechConf also organises leading Women in Tech events across Europe, including the Women in Tech EMEA Summit, taking place in Berlin on 29th and 30th October 2026, and the Women in Tech London Summit. These events bring together senior leaders, technical teams, founders, hiring managers, engineers, recruiters, and partner companies from across the technology ecosystem.



FemTechConf is also behind Women in AI by FemTechConf, one of the world’s largest global communities for women and allies in AI, connecting leaders, engineers, researchers, founders, investors, and organisations shaping the future of artificial intelligence.
