About the job
Ciklum is looking for a Senior Full Stack Engineer to join our team full-time in Ukraine.

We are a custom product engineering company that supports both multinational organizations and scaling startups to solve their most complex business challenges. With a global team of over 4,000 highly skilled developers, consultants, analysts and product owners, we engineer technology that redefines industries and shapes the way people live.



About The Role:

As a Senior Full Stack Engineer, become a part of a cross-functional development team engineering experiences of tomorrow.

The engineer will work closely with product, architecture, AI engineering, and platform teams to build usable, secure, and scalable agent interfaces that connect into enterprise systems, data sources, LLM services, and internal client platform capabilities.



Responsibilities:

Build full stack features for AI agent use cases, including chat interfaces, task review screens, approval flows, exception queues and operational dashboards
Develop React/TypeScript frontend components for agent interaction, configuration, monitoring and human-in-the-loop review
Design and implement backend APIs and services that support agent execution, data retrieval, workflow actions, audit trails and enterprise integrations
Connect agents with enterprise systems, databases, document stores, APIs and event-based workflows used within client environment
Integrate with LLM gateways, model platforms, vector stores, prompt/configuration services and AI observability tooling
Implement secure, production-ready patterns for authentication, RBAC, tenant-aware data access, logging, testing, CI/CD and deployment support 


Requirements:

Strong hands-on experience with React, TypeScript, modern frontend patterns and business application UI development
Backend engineering experience with Python/FastAPI, Java/Spring Boot or Node.js
Experience building REST APIs, integration services and data access layers for enterprise applications
Working knowledge of PostgreSQL or similar relational databases; exposure to vector databases is beneficial
Understanding of AI agent patterns such as tool/function calling, RAG, prompt configuration, workflow orchestration and human-in-the-loop design
Experience with OAuth 2.0, SSO, RBAC, secure API design and multi-tenant application patterns
Familiarity with Git, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes, logging and production troubleshooting 


Desirable:

Experience with LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel, CrewAI, or similar agent frameworks
Exposure to Langfuse, LangSmith, OpenTelemetry, or similar observability tools
Experience with Temporal or workflow orchestration platforms for long-running business processes
Experience building review queues, approval workflows, case management screens, or operational dashboards
Familiarity with AWS services, especially AWS Bedrock, API Gateway, Lambda, S3, and related cloud-native services
Experience working from Figma designs and building reusable UI component libraries
