Job Description

Strategic Imperative:

The strategic imperative of the Sr. Full Stack Engineer is to strengthen the organization’s technical foundation by driving the design and delivery of scalable, resilient, and future-ready systems. As the platform evolves, the ability to support growth, performance, and reliability at enterprise scale is critical. This position plays a central role in translating long-term architectural vision into practical engineering outcomes.

The Senior Full-stack role is expected to actively contribute to modernization initiatives, microservices evolution, and event-driven architecture strategies that enable faster innovation and operational stability. By championing sound engineering principles, architectural consistency, and high-quality implementation, the role ensures that technology investments support business priorities and reduce long-term technical risk.

Equally important is the role’s influence on engineering culture. The strategic value extends beyond individual delivery to strengthening team capability, promoting knowledge sharing, and reinforcing standards that enable sustainable scaling of both systems and people. This position serves as a bridge between architectural direction and execution, ensuring that strategic objectives are realized through disciplined engineering practices.

Ultimately, the strategic imperative is to build systems that are not only effective today but adaptable for tomorrow — enabling the organization to respond confidently to changing market demands, increased scale, and emerging technological opportunities.

Who We Are

BitBurst, a Prodege, LLC company, is a market research company that uses technology to connect people with surveys and provide real-time insights for industries. The company uses its own panels and those of third parties to conduct research and connect targeted audiences with market research globally. BitBurst also offers apps that provide rewarded surveys and other tasks for money, such as gift cards or micro payments.

Primary Objectives

Build and scale modern full-stack product experiences that are performant, reliable, and maintainable across web applications and supporting services.
Translate architectural direction into high-quality execution by designing and implementing distributed systems aligned to enterprise standards and long-term platform strategy.
Advance microservices and event-driven architecture to improve system scalability, decoupling, and speed of iteration.
Ensure data integrity and performance at scale through strong relational modeling, query optimization, and disciplined data access patterns.
Raise engineering quality and reduce technical risk through secure coding practices, code reviews, testing discipline, and strong technical documentation.
Enable reliable delivery and operational excellence by improving CI/CD, deployment safety, observability, and performance tuning. Strengthen team capability and cross-functional execution by mentoring peers and collaborating effectively with Product, QA, DevOps, and Infrastructure.

What does SUCCESS look like?

Success in this role is defined by consistently delivering high-quality, scalable software solutions that align with organizational architecture standards and business objectives. A successful Senior Fullstack Developer demonstrates strong technical ownership, contributes meaningfully to system design decisions, and serves as a reliable technical partner across teams.

Within the first months, success includes developing a deep understanding of the platform, codebase, and architectural landscape, while establishing credibility through thoughtful contributions and dependable delivery. Over time, success is reflected in the ability to design and implement resilient features, optimize system performance, and proactively address technical risks before they impact operations.

A successful candidate elevates team standards through mentorship, constructive code reviews, and advocacy for best practices in software engineering, security, and reliability. They communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both engineering and non-engineering stakeholders and contribute to a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Ultimately, success is measured not only by individual output but by the long-term stability, scalability, and maintainability of the systems they help shape, as well as the positive influence they have on team capability and engineering excellence.
